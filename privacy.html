TravelReel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · applies to the TravelReel app for Android (com.travelreel.app)

Short version: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your journeys, photos and videos stay on your phone.

Data we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Journeys (places, routes, settings) you create
  • Photos you attach to stops (copied into the app's private storage)
  • Videos you render (rendered on-device, saved to your gallery when you choose)

Network requests the app makes

To draw maps, search places and follow roads, the app contacts free public services. These requests contain only what is needed (map tile coordinates, the place name you typed, or the start and end coordinates of a road leg) and no account or device identifiers:

  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles
  • NASA GIBS — satellite imagery (Blue Marble / Black Marble)
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) — place search, and the town name for a stop found in your photos (only its coordinates are sent)
  • routing.openstreetmap.de (FOSSGIS) — road routes

These services may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) under their own privacy policies.

Purchases (TravelReel Pro)

TravelReel Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives from Google Play which plan is active on your account, so it can unlock Pro features. Manage or cancel subscriptions any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Photos / media — only when you pick a photo or save a video
  • Photo location — with “From my trip photos”, the app reads the GPS tags stored in the photos you pick, on your device, to place your stops. It never reads your phone's current location.
  • Internet — maps, search and routing as above

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a journey or video inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.
